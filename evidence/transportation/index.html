CASE EXPLAINER

Transportation context matters

A trip includes reaching, waiting, boarding, riding, transferring, and functioning after arrival—not only vehicle type.

Why transportation context matters

Vehicle movement, posture, route duration, transfer demands, footwear handling, and recovery can change practical access.

What is measured

Wearables estimate movement and body-coupling exposure; route records describe activity and transport context.

What it does not establish

Measurements do not diagnose pain, establish legal entitlement, or make a mode universally safer or better.

Bounded finding: the governed record distinguishes transportation contexts with different measured movement/body-coupling profiles, including the distinction between passive transportation and active motorcycle control.
