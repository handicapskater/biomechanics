CASE SUMMARY

Why longitudinal evidence matters

A long-running personal mobility pattern is more informative than a single episode, while still remaining an N-of-1 record.

QUESTION

Does sustained skating function persist in real-world use over time?

What was observed: governed route, distance, duration, activity, and recovery-context records preserve repeated real-world skating observations.

Time span / scope: longitudinal activity is distinct from episodic RRI, HRV, ECG, and ACC sessions.

Bounded findings: sustained skating function is documented over multiple years; history, route geography, and modern instrumentation are not treated as the same evidence layer.

Important limitation: longitudinal context does not measure pain, prove causation, or turn an individual pattern into universal proof.
