GOVERNED N-OF-1 BIOMECHANICS

Walking is the ballistic mobility perturbation. Rolling skating is a different mechanical strategy.

In the governed repeated protocol, Walking produces greater selected vertical loading, faster acceleration change, and denser impact-like excursions while providing dramatically less functional mobility than controlled Mall skating.

PT skating restores substantial functional mobility afterward in a distinct mechanical and exercise state.
